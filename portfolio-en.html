Privacy

Information you share about your project

Your name, reply email address, project details and other information you provide are used only to respond to your inquiry and communicate about your project.

External services

Video thumbnails may load from YouTube. Selecting a video connects to the YouTube embedded player. Links to Instagram, X, shop websites and other external services are subject to those services' privacy policies.

Inquiries are received through Google Forms and the information submitted is managed using Google's services. Form responses are not published on this website.

Website analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand visits and improve the site. Cookies are used to send browsing data, such as pages viewed, to Google so we can review visitor numbers and how the site is used.

Your inquiry content, name and email address are not sent to Analytics. Advertising personalization is not used.

You can use the site without allowing analytics. Your choice is saved in this browser for 180 days and can be changed at any time using Analytics preferences in the footer. Withdrawing permission removes this site's analytics cookies and stops further collection.
