HOW WE WORK

From first conversation to delivery.

Drawing on my experience listening to corporate clients and preparing proposals, I help clarify your wishes and work through your questions.
Before production begins, we agree on scope, cost, timing and the extent of revisions.

  1. 01

    Talk through your idea

    Tell me what you want to create, where it will be used, any references, your preferred timing and your budget. A rough idea is enough to get started.

  2. 02

    Review the scope & estimate

    I outline the work and project terms, then prepare an estimate. Production starts once you have reviewed and agreed to the details.

  3. 03

    Develop the concept & create

    I shape the story and visual approach, then create the work through the agreed stages, such as image and video generation, editing and sound.

  4. 04

    Review & refine

    You review the work and share your feedback. Adjustments are made within the revision scope agreed in advance.

  5. 05

    Receive your finished work

    Files are delivered in the agreed format for their intended use.
